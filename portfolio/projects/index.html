Murmo

AI 錄音筆記 app，說過的話自動變成筆記。An AI note-taker that turns what you hear into notes.

核心功能：Core features: 邊錄邊出逐字稿，一鍵整理成摘要、重點、待辦和複習卡；Google 登入、雲端同步錄音檔。Live transcription, one-tap summaries, key points, to-dos and review cards; Google sign-in and cloud-synced audio.

外送評論雷達Delivery Review Radar

Chrome 擴充功能：點外送前，自動比對店家的 Google 評論。A Chrome extension that checks a restaurant's Google reviews before you order delivery.

核心功能：Core features: 在 foodpanda / Uber Eats 店家頁自動對到 Google Maps 同一家店，讀近一年評論；衛生警示（蟑螂、異物、吃壞肚子）、衛生／味道／速度／服務四面向評分、推薦與避雷菜色；可選 Claude、ChatGPT、Gemini 或本機 Ollama 模型，紀錄存在本機。Matches foodpanda / Uber Eats restaurants to the same place on Google Maps and reads the past year of reviews; hygiene alerts (bugs, foreign objects, stomach issues), 1–5 scores for hygiene, taste, speed and service, and dishes to order or skip; works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or local Ollama models, with history kept on your device.
